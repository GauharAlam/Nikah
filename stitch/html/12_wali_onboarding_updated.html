The Role of the Wali

Sacred Islamic Guidance

The Prophet ﷺ stated: “There is no marriage without a guardian (Wali).” (Sunan Abi Dawud).

In Islamic jurisprudence, the Wali acts as a protective shield and an advocate for the bride’s best interests, ensuring the prospective partner possesses righteousness, integrity, and compatibility.

Are you a revert or without family mahram?

Under Sharia, the local Islamic Authority (Imam/Qadi) steps forward as your authorized Wali Al-Hakim to ensure you receive full dignified protection.
